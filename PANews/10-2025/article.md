# October 2025 Article
This month in Project Arrhythmia:
- Publisher Parts ways
- Story Mode Playtest
- Object Materials in BetterLegacy
- Heavily Improved Undo
- Continued Work on Asset Packs

## Publisher Parts ways
For a couple of years the publisher of Project Arrhythmia was Top Hat Studios. Just this month, they parted ways with Vitamin Games. More can be read in the Steam post.  

## Story Mode Playtest
As the official story mode release gets closer, randomly picked players from a form submission are able to playtest most of the levels in the story. Again, more info is in the Steam post.  

## Object Materials in BetterLegacy
The BetterLegacy mod now has color blending, actor frame texture, outlines and object textures!  

## Heavily Improved Undo
The undo / redo system (in BetterLegacy) is now capable of a lot more than it ever has been:  
- Expanding a Prefab.
- Deleting a checkpoint.
- Deleting objects now far more stable and can remember deleted parent chains, even of objects that weren't deleted.
- Pasting objects.

## Continued Work on Asset Packs
The Asset Pack system was introduced last month in the 1.8.3 update for BetterLegacy. This system replaces most of the old config settings for editor UI and editor complexity even uses the system.
